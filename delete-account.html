Delete your data

klustafuk does not create an account, so there is no cloud profile to close. Data lives on this device and on the klustafuk computer you pointed it at.

  1. Open klustafuk and go to Safety.
  2. Choose Delete data on this device.
  3. That clears consent and the saved address on the device, and paused-model notes on your control plane. A prompt is not kept after the reply is shown.

The same control is in the Android app under Safety. Deleting data removes it. klustafuk does not freeze an account in its place.
